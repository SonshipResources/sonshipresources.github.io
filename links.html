J. Preston Eby was born into a Mennonite family in Pennsylvania in 1936. He's regarded as the most insightful explorer and prolific author of Bible studies on topics of Sonship, the Manifestation of the Sons of God, Ultimate Reconciliation, and the glorious coming of the Kingdom of God, that the world has ever seen. In addition to many years of pastoral ministry in the U.S. and Latin America, Preston's work over many more decades of deep diving into the Scriptures is impressive indeed.

Though his writings can be lengthy, those blessed with enough time, interest and inquisitive attention to explore them will gain a rich harvest of "nuggets" - a treasure trove of understanding on such "exotic" topics as these as they tread their own path of Sonship in the footsteps of Our Lord Jesus. Though each and every conclusion Preston came to may not be agreed upon by everyone, we can promise that the vast majority will be. No list of resources on Sonship topics could possibly be complete without the great, extensive works of Mr. Eby.
