Join Our Group of Like-Minded Jesus & Sonship Seekers!

  • 100% Free and easy-to-use for PC, Mac, Linux - or Android and iPhone mobiles
  • Very small Download and Install footprint
  • Secure setup; only Email Sign-up required by Mumble; Custom Usernames, you choose
  • Exceptional Voice Quality (plus Texting), with very Low Latency

Why You Might Like This Casual Chat?

  • This path can be a lonely one. Maybe it's time for a little like-minded Friendship and Communion. Yes?
  • There's mutual support to be had here - and, dare we say, Fun? - certainly Prayer Support as Needed
  • We believe the Lord is going to Bless this Endeavor. Good and Powerful things will therefore Result!

    • Rules - Only a Few

    • Be Kind to One Another above All. We are not going to Agree on absolutely Everything.
    • Friendly Discussion is Welcome and Encouraged - DEBATE IS NOT.
    • No Politics! (due to its Polarizing nature). If you must, please discuss in a Private Room.
    • If you are on Computer, you Must Use Headphones. The Echo without this is terrible.
    • We ask that you keep your language Mature and generally Family-Friendly.
