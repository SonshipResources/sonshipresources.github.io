The Kingdom Of God

Kingdom Bible Studies

J. Preston Eby was born into a Mennonite family in Pennsylvania in 1936. He's regarded as the most insightful explorer and prolific author of Bible study on topics of Sonship, the Manifestation of the Sons of God, and the coming Kingdom of God, that the world has ever seen. In addition to many years of pastoral ministry in the U.S. and Latin America, Preston's work over many more decades of deep diving into the Scriptures are impressive.

Though his writings can be lengthy, those blessed with enough time, interest and inquisitive attention to explore them will harvest a rich collection of "nuggets" - a treasure chest of understanding of these "exotic" topics better as they tread the path of Sonship, even in the footsteps of Our Lord Jesus. Though each and every single conclusion Preston came to may not be agreed to by everyone, we can promise that the vast majority are. No list of resources on these topics could be complete without the great works of J. Preston Eby.
