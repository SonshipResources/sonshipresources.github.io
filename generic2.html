The Kingdom Of God

Kingdom Bible Studies

J. Preston Eby was born into a Mennonite family in Lancaster County, Pennsylvania in 1936. He is regarded as the most prolific writer and explorer of insightful Bible Studies on topics of Sonship, the Manifestation of the Sons of God and the coming Kingdom of God the world has ever seen. In addition to many years of pastoral ministry service in the U.S. and abroad, his work over numerous decades of profound exploration in the Holy Scriptures could fill a small barn!

Though his writings are lengthy, those blessed enough with the time, interest and inquisitive focus to dig into them, will receive a rich collection of life-changing "nuggets" along the path of Sonship as we pursue the way of Our Lord Jesus. Though each and every conclusion Preston came to may not fully agree with every seeker, we can promise that by far the majority will, if you've found yourself awakened to the truths of full-blown Sonship according to the Scriptures. No list of resources on the topic could be complete without pointing out these great works.
