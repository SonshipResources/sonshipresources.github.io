The Feast Of Tabernacles

Widely considered the essential theological basis for the "Manifestation of the Sons of God" and the Lord's Fulfillment of the Ages regarding His ultimate "Church," the Body of Christ, and His future Kingdom of rulership and divine governance upon the earth as we know it. This theology also leads to God's purpose being potentially to redeem the entire race of mankind (but this is probably better left to other discussions). Suffice it to say that this mightily inspired work contains the entire Biblical basis for the Tidal Wave of God's justice, power, blessing & restoration eventually coming to our planet.

George's dense little book is a thorough, eye-opening Bible study of all the Scriptures, outlining every connected theme and prophecy of this greatest of all Moves God has planned for us in the earth - anchored and paid for by the divine life, crucifixion and resurrection of Our Lord Jesus Christ 2,000 years ago.

Mr. Warnock was born in 1917 in North Battleford, Saskatchewan, Canada and was part of the Latter Rain Movement of the late 1940's and 1950's there. His website hosts many valuable studies, but The Feast Of Tabernacles is by far the most important.
