The Feast Of Tabernacles

This free 1951 book by George Warnock is widely considered the essential theological basis for the "Manifestation of the Sons of God" and the Lord's Fulfillment of the Ages through His ultimate "Church" - the Body of Christ - and His future Kingdom of rulership and divine governance upon the earth as we know it. This theology also potentially leads to God's purpose being to redeem the entire race of mankind (but this is probably best left to later discussions). Suffice it to say that George's mightily inspired work contains the entire Biblical basis for the Tidal Wave of God's justice, power, blessing & restoration eventually coming across our planet. This is represented in the OT by the final (as yet unfulfilled) feast, Tabernacles.

This dense little treatise is a thorough, eye-opening Bible study of all of the Scriptures, outlining every connected theme and prophecy of this greatest of all moves of God, that He's always had planned for us - anchored and paid for by the divine life, crucifixion and resurrection of Our Lord Jesus Christ 2,000 years ago.

Mr. Warnock was born in 1917 in North Battleford, Saskatchewan, Canada and was part of the Latter Rain Movement of the late 1940's and 1950's there. His website hosts many valuable studies, but The Feast Of Tabernacles is his most important work.
