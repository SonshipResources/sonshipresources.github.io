Signs of the Times

Aware that every generation for the past 2,000 years has believed they were living in the last days, we are nevertheless hard-pressed not to notice the signs of the times roiling all around us. Have they ever seemed so ripe? Have we ever seen such swift change in what could still be called relative "peacetime" - but is not?

And those who felt destined, maybe even prepared, to bear a move of God so huge the world has never seen it nor believed it possible - maybe these few who've known that it is have been made a little more ready. Some were scattered to the winds, or hidden away in forgotten prayer closets, active in ministry or not - outside all these years, or still inside the "camp" of mainstream Christianity, true followers of Our Lord Jesus wherever they be hidden or found. It doesn't matter. The searing blast of the Refiner's fire has reached them and done Its job wherever they took refuge. And lovingly brought them through it all to this very day and time.

So yes the whirlwind of change in the world is a resounding call to many... and to the few, who with long-waiting hearts and minds, attentive to their Lord's growing signal, may hear it the loudest. The times are ripe with recognition of just where we are in History. Did we not come here for this, beloved? Can we not feel the birth-pangs growing? Can we not hear the Almighty calling us to attention?
