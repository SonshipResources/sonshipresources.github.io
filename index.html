Signs of the Times

Aware that every generation for the past 2,000 years has believed they were living in the last days, we are nevertheless hard-pressed not to notice the signs of the times roiling all around us. Have they ever seemed so ripe? Have we ever seen such swift change in what could still be called relative "peacetime" - but is not?

And those who felt destined, maybe even prepared, to bear a move of God so huge the world has never seen it nor believed it possible - even these destined few have become more and more ready. Some were scattered to the wind, or hidden away in long-forgotten prayer closets, active in ministry or not - outside all these years, or inside the "camp" of mainstream Christianity, true followers of Our Lord Jesus wherever they are hidden or found.

The whirlwind of change brings a resounding call to some ears... long-awaiting ears, still alert, attentive to their Lord's growing signals. And the times are ripe with recognition of where we are in History. We came here for this, Beloved! Can you feel the birth-pangs? Can you hear the clarion Call?
