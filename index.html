Signs of the Times

Aware that every generation for the past 2,000 years has believed they were living in the last days, we are nevertheless hard-pressed not to notice the signs of the times roiling all around us today. Have they ever seemed so ripe as now? Have we ever seen such swift change in what could still be called "peacetime?"

And those who have felt destined, even gradually prepared to see and bear a move of God on a scale the world has never seen nor believed possible... become more and more ready for it. Those who have often seemed scattered to the wind, or hidden away in long-forgotten prayer closets - outside, or perhaps even inside the main "camp" of mainstream Christian believers and followers of Our Lord Jesus.

But the whirlwind of change brings a clarion call to some ears... long-awaiting ears that have stayed alert, attentive to the Lord's signals. And the times are ripe indeed with a recognition of where we are in History. We came here for this, Beloved! Can you feel it? Can you hear the Calling?
