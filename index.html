Signs of the Times

Aware that every generation for the past 2,000 years has believed they were living in the End Times, we are still hard-pressed NOT to notice the signs of the times roiling up all around us. Have they ever seemed so ripe? Maybe. But have we ever seen such swift change in land and sea and society in what could be called a relative "peacetime" - but is anything but?

And those who felt destined, maybe even prepared, to bear a move of God so Huge the world has never seen it nor believed in it - maybe these few who've known it as much more than only possible were simply made more ready by the waiting for it. Some seemed scattered to the four winds, or hidden away in a forgotten prayer closet, active in ministry or not - outside, all these years, or still inside the "camp" of mainstream Christianity - true followers of Our Lord Jesus wherever hidden or found. It doesn't matter. Surely the blast of the Refiner's fire has searched them out carefully to carry out Its deadly work - an uncomfortable qualification for true Sonship. Crucified! Yet in love, their day approaches, refined as Gold for this very hour!

Yes, the Whirlwind of change in the world is a resounding call to many... and to the few, with longing hearts tuned to the Lord's increasing signals, it may even be heard the loudest. The times are ripe with recognition of where we are in History. Did we not come here for this, beloved? Can we not feel the birth-pangs? Can we not hear the Almighty calling us to attention?
